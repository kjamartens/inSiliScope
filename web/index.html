Cell field — seed→coordinate mock

Chunk-hashed, address-based placement (pcg4d, ported from webSMLM / demoCam_SMLM_MM's CounterRng). Every cell/microtubule is a pure function of (seed, chunk coords) — nothing is generated ahead of time, so you can pan infinitely and re-enter the same spot deterministically. Not wired into webSMLM itself — standalone exploration only.
