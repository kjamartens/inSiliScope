Voxelize ground truth

Turns a ground-truth table of inSiliScope (dyes.csv or a localizations_min*.csv) into a density stack at the voxel size you choose: the sum of a column (emitted photons, by default) per voxel, or a count; one stack, or one channel per target. Fiji/ImageJ and napari read its voxel size; Fiji also its world origin, so the status bar shows each voxel's world x, y and z (µm). 8 and 16 bits scale each channel to its maximum: the photons per step are in the description (insiliscope.scale), and a single-channel stack carries them as its calibration. Everything runs in this page: nothing is uploaded.
