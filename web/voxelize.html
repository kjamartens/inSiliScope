Voxelize ground truth

Turns a ground-truth table of inSiliScope (dyes.csv or a localizations_min*.csv) into a 32-bit density stack at the voxel size you choose: the sum of a column (emitted photons, by default) per voxel, or a count. The stack opens in Fiji/ImageJ and napari with its voxel size; its world origin is in the image description (insiliscope.origin-um). Everything runs in this page: nothing is uploaded.
