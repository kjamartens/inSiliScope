Label linkage

Where each label's dyes sit relative to their binding sites (scope/dyes.js linkedDye): a binder of length b along the structure's axis (a microtubule: radially out of the lattice; a pore: its corner's radial direction in the ring plane), tilted within a cone, then a linker of length uniform in volume in [min, max]. The presets are data/dyes/library.json linkagePresets; Legacy is each structure's numbers before the model.
