Where each label's dyes sit relative to their binding sites (scope/dyes.js linkedDye), in three parts: the binder (the probe: antibodies, a nanobody, a tag, a fluorescent protein) reaches b from the site along the structure's axis (a microtubule: radially out of the lattice; a pore: its corner's direction in the ring plane), its flexibility moves its end anywhere in a ball of radius f; the chemical linker (length uniform in volume in [min, max], direction uniform) carries the dye. Presets: data/dyes/library.json linkagePresets (sources and estimates in their notes); Legacy is each structure's numbers before the model.
